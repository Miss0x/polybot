Will Aleksandar Vučić become the next Prime Minister of Serbia?

佐证：Vučić's resignation as president and pivot to PM candidacy (Sep 28) signals intention, and his SNS party leads multiple polls (35.7%-48.6% across CRTA, NSPM, Faktor Plus). However, the Student-Led challenge (STU polling 37.1%-44.9% in CRTA) is highly competitive and the outcome is uncertain given the wide polling spread. No poll directly tests PM preference, and election results are not yet available.

反方最强：CRTA (most methodologically rigorous per its independent-observer status) shows STU at 44.9% versus SNS at 35.7%, suggesting the student-led coalition could plausibly outpoll SNS and prevent a Vučić PM bid.

翻转条件：A post-resignation poll showing either party clearly above 50% with consistent cross-pollster methodology, or a pre-election coalition agreement between STU and opposition parties (DS, NADA, etc.) that would consolidate the anti-SNS vote.

市场参考价：67.0 分（对照用，非本页结论）
